## Why this song is here

- It's **the** JustinGuitar first song — Module 1 is literally titled "A & D Chords + Play Your First Song!"
- Uses **only A and D** — the exact two chords and the A→D change you already locked (18/20 at 60 BPM). Nothing new to learn on the fretting side; this is purely about *playing a song*.
- A recognizable, fun tune — the whole point of "progress over polish": a real song in your hands right now, not after five more modules.

## What you're building

- The A↔D change **inside a song** — switching on time with the music, not just in isolation drills.
- Steady strumming while changing chords — the coordination that makes a change feel musical.
- Playing to a pulse (backing track or metronome) rather than stop-start.

## 🎯 Focus when working on it

- **Chord-notes check first** (per the routine): A = A–E–A–C♯–E (skip string 6); D = D–A–D–F♯ (skip 6, 5).
- Start with **down-strums only**, one per beat, well under tempo. Change A↔D on the beat.
- Don't chase 126 BPM — play it slow and in time. A slow, steady run-through beats a fast, stumbling one.
- Once the change flows with down-strums, add the universal strum (D-D-U-U-D-U) if it's comfortable — but rhythm-clean-and-slow is the win, not speed.
- If the A-chord D-string buzz shows up, that's the known review item — quick reseat, keep going.
